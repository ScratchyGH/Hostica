    Welcome to Hostica
    

    

    

    

    


    Build & host websites in your browser
    

    No server. No setup. Everything runs locally.
    

    

    

    1
    Create a project
    Click New on the dashboard. Pick a template or start blank.
    

    2
    Edit your files
    The editor supports HTML, CSS, JS, TypeScript, Python, Rust, Go, C++, Lua, SQL and more. Changes save automatically.
    

    3
    Preview live
    Click Preview for a side-by-side view, or Play for fullscreen.
    

    

    

    

    

    Sharing & publishing
    

    4
    Share your project
    Click Share in the editor. Choose a visibility type and publish to the Gun.js network.
    

    5
    Three visibility types
    Public — Searchable in Community, hostable via share link.
    Unlisted — Hostable but not in search results.
    Private — Stays local, no sharing.
    

    6
    Two share links
    View + code shows your project with a file browser. Site only shows just your site — like a real hosted URL.
    

    

    

    

    

    Community & advanced features
    

    7
    Browse Community
    Click Community in the sidebar to discover public projects, filter by language, sort by stars, and fork anything you like.
    

    8
    Import projects
    Drag a .zip or .html file onto the Files panel in the editor, or use Import on the dashboard.
    

    9
    Backend languages via WASM
    Python, Lua, and SQL run directly in the browser via WebAssembly. TypeScript, JSX, SCSS, Svelte, Markdown, and CoffeeScript compile on the fly.
    

    

    All data is stored locally in your browser. Your account never leaves your device. Projects you publish are stored on the Gun.js decentralized network.
